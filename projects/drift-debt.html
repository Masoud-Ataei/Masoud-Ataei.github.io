Drift Debt Simulator

A ship flies home with exactly enough trip fuel. Its left engine (Engine A) cuts out at random, the ship drifts off course, and side thrusters must burn reserve fuel to fix it, but only while Engine A is running. Set the conditions, launch the flight, and see whether the reserve lasts.
